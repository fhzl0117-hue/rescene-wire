SEOUL — OCTOBER 2026

A Scent That
Won't Fade.

RESCENE is a five-member girl group under THE MUZE Entertainment — Woni, Liv, Minami, May, and Zena — built around a "fragrance" concept: music designed to linger the way a scent does. They debuted quietly in March 2024 and spent two years mostly under the radar, until a 2026 viral clip changed everything: leader Woni's personal YouTube channel posted a video of her and Japanese member Minami visiting Woni's hometown of Geoje, where Minami's gyaru-style shout of "Geoje Yaho!" turned into a Gen Z meme with millions of views. The wave pulled 2024's "Love Attack" back onto the charts, led to a surprise cameo from KARA's Nicole on remake single "Pretty Girl," and gave the group its first-ever music show win in July. A fourth mini album, Pulse, lands November 3 — the group's first physical release in about a year. This page is a working build of how RESCENE Wire tracks all of it for English-speaking REMINE — translated, sourced, and converted to whatever timezone you're reading from.

A note on why this site exists now: RESCENE's agency, THE MUZE Entertainment, is one of ten small and mid-size companies selected for a new government-backed program supporting K-pop agencies' overseas expansion, announced June 2026. The group has said directly that it wants to "climb higher and expand globally" — this page starts that English-language coverage early, before the overseas fandom catches up to the domestic one.

Desk 02 · Review

Revisiting the catalogue, track by track.

"Non-Skip"THE K MEAL
"A mini album filled with charming tracks — a non-skip experience from start to finish."

"Scenedrome" (2024): RESCENE's debut EP, built around a dark, mysterious opener and a hook-driven title track. The K Meal's Kathleen Herrera praised opener "Lucky You" for its heavy synths, impressive beat drops, and layered vocals, calling it "a major highlight," then singled out title track "Love Attack" as "a solid title track that showcases the group's talents," with a hook she called "incredible." "New World" takes an R&B turn she described as "pure vibes," while closer "Pinball" lands a smooth, dreamy finish she said "leaves a lasting impression."

Two years later, "Love Attack" is the song that actually broke through — not on release week, but in 2026, after a viral meme pulled it back onto the charts. The review's praise for the hook holds up: it's the same hook that eventually got RESCENE their resurgence.
